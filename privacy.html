Privacy Policy

Effective: October 1, 2026

This policy explains the information Paradise SMP may process through its Minecraft server, Discord bot, plugin and website.

1. Information we process

Depending on the features you use, the system may process Minecraft usernames and UUIDs, Discord user IDs, account-link information, whitelist state, gameplay statistics such as playtime, kills and deaths, in-game economy data, event participation, moderation records, ticket/report submissions and technical server activity.

2. Public information

The public website may display server status, online player names and leaderboard information such as player names, balances, kills, deaths or playtime. Precise in-game coordinates are not published through the public website or public Discord player profiles. Do not use a Minecraft username on Paradise SMP if you are not comfortable with server-related public statistics being associated with that username.

3. Why information is used

Information is used to operate account linking, staff-managed whitelist access, moderation, support, leaderboards, live server status, community features, security, analytics and other Paradise SMP functionality.

4. Logs and retention

Operational and moderation records may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to run the community, enforce rules, resolve disputes, diagnose technical issues and protect the service. Staff may remove or archive old records as server policies change.

5. Sharing

Paradise SMP does not need to sell personal information to operate this system. Information may be processed by infrastructure providers used to host the Minecraft server, Discord bot, website and database, and may be disclosed when required for security, abuse handling or legal obligations.

6. Security

The bot/plugin bridge uses a shared secret and staff actions are permission-controlled. No online system can guarantee absolute security, so access credentials and administrative secrets should be kept private and rotated if exposed.

7. Your choices

You can stop using Paradise SMP services at any time. Requests about account-link records or other stored community data should be sent to Paradise SMP staff through the official support process. Some moderation or security records may need to be retained where reasonably necessary.

8. Third-party platforms

Minecraft, Microsoft, Discord and hosting providers have their own privacy practices. This policy only describes Paradise SMP's use of information in its own connected systems.

9. Changes

This policy may change as features are added or removed. The effective date will be updated when a revised version is published.
